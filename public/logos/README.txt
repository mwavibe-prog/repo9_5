Logos folder.

Drop these two files in here for the logo bar at the top of the host and
player screens to display:

  crest-logo.png       - Crest Secondary logo
  weeswares-logo.png   - WeesWares logo

PNG is preferred (transparent background works best). If you use a
different format or name, update the `src="/logos/..."` references in
public/index.html and public/play.html accordingly.
